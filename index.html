Why it's built this way

Three things that don't change no matter which business sits on top.

—01

One tenant boundary, enforced everywhere

Every service, from the messaging engine to the agent desk, resolves the organization first — a water-purifier dealer's data and a boutique franchise's data never share a query.

—02

Events, not polling

A delivery receipt, a form submission, a wallet credit — each is an event the moment it happens, so automations and the agent desk react in seconds, not on the next cron tick.

—03

We eat what we cook

Both businesses above run their actual service calendars and invoices on this stack. A bug in the platform is a bug in our own operations first — that's a sharper incentive than a staging environment.
